See NutriScan in Action

From snapping your meal to tracking every nutrient — see how it all works.

Snap Your Meal

Open NutriScan and tap the camera button to take a photo of any meal. The app instantly analyzes what's on your plate.

No manual searching or typing — just point, snap, and get your nutrition insights in seconds.

Instant Scan Results

NutriScan identifies your meal and shows calories, protein, fat, and carbs at a glance right below the photo.

Adjust servings with a tap, and scroll down for the full Nutrition Facts — all from a single snap.

Full Nutrition Facts

Get a complete Nutrition Facts label for your meal — from calories and macros to vitamins and minerals.

Values are clearly flagged as AI estimates with tap-through sources, so you always know where the numbers come from.

Detailed Nutrient Breakdown

Tap "View full nutrient list" to see every nutrient in your meal — energy, water, fatty acids, fiber, and more.

Sort by name or amount and tap for AI sources, giving you the deepest level of nutritional detail.

Your Daily Dashboard

See your daily calories, Health Score, and macro progress bars all in one clean view.

Instantly know how your day is tracking against your goals and jump to the full nutrient list for more detail.

Monthly Calendar View

Review your month at a glance with color-coded rings for Energy and Health Score on every day.

Tap any date to see that day's Health Score, calorie total, and full macro breakdown.

Daily Meal Log

Scroll down on any calendar day to see every meal you logged — complete with photos and timestamps.

Get a full picture of what you ate alongside your macro totals for the day.

Meal History & Search

Browse all your past meals organized by day, or use the search bar to find a specific dish instantly.

Tap any entry to revisit its full nutrition details — your entire food journal, always at your fingertips.

Home Screen Widget

Track your daily calories, protein, carbs, and fat at a glance — right from your home screen.

The NutriScan widget keeps your macro progress visible without even opening the app.
